Quick answers

Import cost per unit
FAQ.

What should be included in import cost per unit?

Include the purchase cost plus the order-level costs needed to get the goods to your usable destination, such as freight, insurance, duty, customs, delivery and other known charges.

Should selling fees be included?

Usually not if you are measuring sourcing or landed cost. Add marketplace, fulfillment and selling costs later when calculating margin or target selling price.

Should I use ordered quantity or sellable quantity?

Ordered quantity is the standard sourcing view. If a meaningful share will be samples, damaged or otherwise unsellable, using expected sellable quantity gives a more conservative commercial cost per unit.
