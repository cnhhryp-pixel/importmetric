Quick answers

DDP landed cost
FAQ.

Does DDP mean the buyer has no extra costs?

Not necessarily. Commercial costs such as receiving, inspection, payment fees, warehousing or internal delivery can still exist even when the seller handles the main import and delivery obligations.

Why should I confirm who is importer of record?

Because importer-of-record arrangements can affect documentation, tax treatment, compliance responsibility and whether a quoted DDP structure is workable in the destination market.

Can I compare DDP with FOB or CIF?

Yes, but compare all options at the same final delivery point and include every buyer-side cost that differs between the terms.
