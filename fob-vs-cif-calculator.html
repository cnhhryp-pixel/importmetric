FOB and CIF are
different scopes.

FOB and CIF allocate transport responsibilities differently, so the quoted unit prices are not directly comparable. This calculator adds the buyer-paid costs needed to bring both quotes to a similar pre-duty destination basis.

Import duty and tax are not included. Customs valuation and tax treatment vary by jurisdiction, so use the Import Duty Calculator or Landed Cost Calculator after normalizing the quote.
