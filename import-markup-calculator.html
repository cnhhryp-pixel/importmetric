Markup vs margin

Two percentages,
two different bases.

Markup measures profit relative to cost. Profit margin measures profit relative to selling price. A 50% markup does not produce a 50% profit margin.

Selling priceCost per unit × (1 + markup rate)
Profit margin(Selling price − cost) ÷ selling price × 100

Quick answers

Import markup
FAQ.

What is the difference between markup and margin?

Markup measures profit relative to cost. Margin measures profit relative to selling price, so the percentages are different even when dollar profit is the same.

What cost should I use as the markup base?

Use the all-in cost relevant to your pricing decision, ideally including landed cost and any fixed per-unit costs that must be recovered through the selling price.

Does a 50% markup equal a 50% margin?

No. A 50% markup on cost produces a margin of about one third of the selling price before any additional percentage fees.
