Quick answers

Supplier price
comparison FAQ.

Why can the supplier with the lower unit price cost more?

MOQ and order-level charges can outweigh a small unit-price advantage. Comparing total order cost and effective cost per unit makes that difference visible.

What should I put under extra order costs?

Use costs that are known and comparable across both suppliers, such as freight, inspection, documentation, duty estimates or bank charges.

Should I compare quotes with different Incoterms here?

Only after normalizing them to a similar delivery basis. For FOB versus CIF specifically, use the FOB vs CIF Calculator first.
