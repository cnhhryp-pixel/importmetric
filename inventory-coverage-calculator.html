Months of cover divides available inventory by expected monthly sales. Lead-time demand estimates how many units could be consumed while waiting for the incoming order.

Current months of coverCurrent inventory ÷ expected monthly sales
Estimated lead-time demandExpected monthly sales × lead time days ÷ 30
