Supplier Quote Checklist: What to Ask Before Comparing Prices

A supplier quote is only comparable when the product specification and commercial scope are clear. This checklist helps expose missing information before price comparison.

Quick answer

Do not compare prices until you know the suppliers are quoting the same thing.

Specification, packaging, quantity, Incoterm, lead time and payment terms should be normalized before a low unit price is treated as a real advantage.

Product specification

Make the physical product comparable first.

Material / gradeDimensions and tolerancesColor / finishWeight or thicknessLogo / printing methodPackaging specificationCompliance requirementsApproved sample reference

If suppliers are quoting different materials, packaging or tolerances, a price comparison is not meaningful.

Commercial terms

Capture the numbers around the unit price.

Unit priceCurrencyMOQPrice-break quantitiesTooling / setup chargesSample chargesPayment termsQuote validity period

Logistics scope

Know exactly where the supplier's responsibility ends.

IncotermNamed place / portCarton quantityCarton dimensionsGross / net weightCBMFreight included or excludedExport documentation scope

“FOB” without a named port and “DDP” without a named delivery place are incomplete for comparison.

Quality and timing

Price is only useful if the product can arrive as required.

Sample lead timeProduction lead timeCapacity for target quantityInspection arrangementDefect handlingWarranty / replacement policyPackaging approval timingShipping-ready date

Comparison workflow

Standardize, normalize, then calculate.

01

Put every quote into the same fields

Use a template so missing terms are obvious.

02

Normalize currency and quantity

Do not compare a 500-unit quote with a 3,000-unit quote without considering the quantity effect.

03

Normalize Incoterms

Add the buyer-paid costs required to reach the same destination point.

04

Compare effective cost and risk

Review MOQ, lead time and cash exposure beside the normalized cost.
