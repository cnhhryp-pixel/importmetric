The shipping math

Move from freight quote
to unit economics.

Shipment quotes are usually given as totals. Dividing the complete logistics total by the planned quantity makes it easier to compare the impact on each unit.

Logistics cost / unit(Origin + freight + insurance + clearance + destination + other) ÷ quantity

This tool does not include import duty unless you enter it under Other Logistics Costs. Use the Landed Cost Calculator when you want the full import-cost view.
