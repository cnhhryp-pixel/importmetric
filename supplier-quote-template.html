What should a supplier
quote template include?

Commercial fields

Record supplier name, unit price, MOQ, Incoterm and lead time so the commercial terms are visible next to the price.

Cost fields

Add freight, duty or tax assumptions and other known charges. This makes it easier to identify which quote still needs normalization before comparison.

Keep specifications separate

Use the Notes field for product or packaging differences, but confirm that suppliers are quoting the same specification before treating the prices as directly comparable.

Quick answers

Supplier quote template
FAQ.

Why should every supplier use the same columns?

Standard fields make missing information visible and reduce the risk of comparing quotes that use different delivery terms, quantities or cost assumptions.

Can I download the table?

Yes. The browser tool can download the current table as CSV, which you can open in spreadsheet software or keep with your sourcing records.

Should I compare prices before confirming specifications?

No. Make sure product, packaging, materials and quality requirements are equivalent first. A lower price for a different specification is not a true price comparison.
