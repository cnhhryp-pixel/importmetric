The pricing math

Percentage fees change
the break-even point.

If a marketplace or payment fee is charged as a percentage of selling price, simply adding that percentage to your fixed cost understates the price required to break even.

Break-even selling priceFixed cost / (1 − selling fee rate)
Price for target marginFixed cost / (1 − selling fee rate − target margin)

This is a planning calculation. Add advertising, returns, taxes and other variable costs when they materially affect your unit economics.

Quick answers

Break-even price
FAQ.

Why can I not just add the marketplace fee percentage to cost?

Because a percentage fee is charged on the selling price, not on your cost base. Solving for price requires dividing fixed cost by one minus the fee rate.

Should advertising and returns be included?

Yes when they are material and reasonably estimable per unit. Leaving them out makes the break-even result too low.

Is break-even price the price I should actually sell at?

Not usually. It is the approximate floor that covers the entered costs. A commercial price should also account for profit, demand, competition and risk.
