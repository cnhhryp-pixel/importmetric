Quick answers

Target selling price
FAQ.

Why must fee rate plus target margin stay below 100%?

Because both are percentages of the selling price. If together they reach or exceed 100%, no portion of the selling price remains to cover fixed unit costs.

Should landed cost include duty and freight?

Yes. Use a true landed or all-in sourcing cost per unit so the target price is based on the full import economics rather than supplier price alone.

Is the calculated target price guaranteed to work in the market?

No. It is a cost-and-margin target. You still need to test demand, competition, channel fees and customer willingness to pay.
