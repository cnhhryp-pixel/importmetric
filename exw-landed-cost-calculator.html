Quick answers

EXW landed cost
FAQ.

Why can EXW look cheaper than FOB?

EXW usually leaves more origin-side logistics with the buyer. Factory pickup, export handling and related charges may not be included in the quoted product price.

Should export handling be added separately?

Yes when it is not included in the supplier price. Confirm who pays pickup, export clearance, terminal handling and other origin charges.

Does this calculator determine the correct customs value?

No. It uses a simplified planning basis. Actual customs valuation depends on destination-country rules and the shipment documentation.
